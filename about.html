Our founder's story

From hardship to hope

Dorcas did not grow up with much. Raised by a single mother in difficult circumstances, her childhood was marked by hardship, instability and pain no child should ever have to carry. At 17, facing a home environment that had become unsafe, she made the difficult decision to leave — alone, with nothing but her faith to hold onto.

Two years later, at 19, Dorcas found herself pregnant. She gave birth in poor conditions, with little support around her, and the experience was so traumatic that she ended up in intensive care. It was in that moment of vulnerability — stripped of everything — that something shifted.

She describes it as a dream from God. A calling she couldn't ignore. A quiet but unmistakable sense that everything she had been through was not the end of her story, but the beginning of something far greater.

Dorcas doesn't help from a distance. She sits with the people she serves. She goes into the communities, looks people in the eye, and gives from the heart — because she knows what it feels like to have nothing and to need someone to show up. Her faith is not something she preaches and walks away from. It is something she lives, every single day, through every kit distributed, every child fed, every elderly woman visited.

What began as one woman's response to her own hardship has grown into a Foundation that has reached over 230 people across Uganda — and Dorcas is only just getting started.

Our faith

Why "the Samaritan"?

In the parable, a traveller is left wounded by the roadside. Others pass by — but the Samaritan stops, binds his wounds and pays for his care.

That is the heart of our work. We give practically alongside sharing the Gospel, so no one is left with words alone.

The people behind the work

Meet our team

Dorcas

Founder

Dorcas founded Dee the Samaritan Charity Foundation in October 2025, out of her own story. She left home alone at 17 and became a young mother at 19 with almost no support, so she knows what it is to have nothing and to need someone to show up. Out of that experience came a calling she couldn’t ignore, and the Foundation was born.

There is no part of the work she doesn’t touch. She visits the communities herself, sitting with the mothers, children and elderly women the Foundation serves. She raises the funds that make every kit and food pack possible, and she sets the vision for where the Foundation goes next, all rooted in the call of Luke 10:37: “Go and do likewise.” Under her leadership, the Foundation has already reached more than 230 people across Kayunga and Wakiso, with plans to grow right across Uganda and beyond.

Asiimwe

Field Operations Coordinator (FOC)

Based on the ground in Uganda, Asiimwe is the heartbeat of the Foundation’s day-to-day operations. She travels into the communities every month, identifying families in need, distributing supplies and ensuring that every kit, food pack and resource reaches the people it was intended for.

She is the eyes and ears of Dee the Samaritan — the person who sits with the mothers, checks on the children and builds the relationships that make this work possible. Without her presence on the ground, none of what we do would reach the people who need it most.
