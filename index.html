Our story

Born from one mother's story

Dorcas did not grow up with much. Raised by a single mother, she left an unsafe home alone at 17 with nothing but her faith. At 19 she gave birth in poor conditions and ended up in intensive care — and in that moment, she heard God's call.

That calling became Dee the Samaritan Charity Foundation, founded in October 2025. Today it walks alongside young mothers, orphaned children, pregnant women and elderly women across Kayunga and Wakiso.

What we do

Seven ways we restore hope

From Mama Kits for expectant mothers to exercise books for rural pupils — every programme meets a real, practical need.

Our heart programme

Teen Mothers & Their Babies

Food, soap, milk and ongoing support for teenage and young mothers raising children alone. It's where our founder's own story began — and it remains at the heart of everything we do.

FoodSoapMilkOngoing mentoring
Support a young mother Learn more

Orphaned & Vulnerable Children

Food, clothing and essentials for orphaned babies and children aged 0–13 with no parental care — whatever their gender.

Pregnant Women — Mama Kits

Birth kits with everything Ugandan health centres require for a safe delivery, for pregnant women and teenagers.

Child Healthcare & Nutrition

Porridge, rice, soy, milk and medicine for malnourished children aged 0–13, found through community networks.

Education & Scholastic Support

Exercise books, pencils and supplies handed personally to pupils in rural schools, plus sanitary items where needed.

Vulnerable Elderly Women

Visits and essential supplies for elderly women living in poor conditions — working towards housing support as funding grows.

Christian Outreach

Our faith runs through all we do — we give practically alongside sharing the Gospel, so no one is left with words alone.
