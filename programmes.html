Teen Mothers & Their Babies

Our founder, Dorcas, became a mother at 19 with little support around her. She knows how isolating it is to raise a child alone, often after being abandoned by a partner or turned away by family.

This programme gives teenage and young mothers the practical essentials they need for their babies — and the ongoing encouragement to rebuild their lives with hope.

What we provide

Food · Soap · Milk · Ongoing support

Who we help

Teenage & young mothers raising children alone

Orphaned & Vulnerable Children

Children aged 0–13 with no parental care, of any gender

We provide food, clothing and essential supplies directly to orphaned babies and children who have no one to care for them — so they can grow up safe, fed and loved.

Pregnant Women — Mama Kits

Pregnant women and teenagers

Ugandan health centres ask expectant mothers to bring their own birth supplies. Many simply can't. Our Mama Kits contain everything required for a safe, clean delivery.

What we provide

SoapFlannelsWipesGlovesReceiving sheetWaterproof matCord threadSurgical blade
