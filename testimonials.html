Child Healthcare & Nutrition

Elvis

Elvis is two years old, but he can barely walk. His mother was only 13 when he was born, and she left him in the care of her grandmother, who is not able to look after him.

Elvis is severely malnourished. The family has almost nothing to eat. Most weeks they eat only about three times, on the days someone shows them mercy and brings them food.

Children like Elvis are why our Child Healthcare & Nutrition programme exists: to make sure no child grows up hungry and forgotten.
